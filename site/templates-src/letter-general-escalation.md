# Template — General Escalation Letter (send to supervisor, corporate, or regulator)

> The stair-step letter for Chapter 2's ladder. Fill every `{{slot}}`; attach
> your first letter and all case numbers. This is rung 2+ — shorter and firmer.

```
Date:           {{DAY-MONTH-YEAR}}
To:             {{MANAGER / CORPORATE COMPLAINTS / REGULATOR OFFICE NAME}}
From:           {{YOUR NAME}}, {{TOWN}}
Subject:        Second request — reference #{{CASE #}} — reply requested by {{DATE +7 DAYS}}

Dear {{NAME or Sir/Madam}},

I'm following up on reference #{{CASE #}}, which I raised on {{DATE OF LETTER 1}} and have heard nothing about. This is my second letter, and I'm requesting it be reviewed at your level.

Facts:
1. On {{DATE OF PURCHASE / INCIDENT}}, I paid ${{AMOUNT}} for {{ITEM / SERVICE}}. Receipt attached.
2. On {{DATE OF FIRST COMPLAINT}}, I wrote to your front-line team asking for {{ORIGINAL REMEDY: "a full refund"}} (copy attached, letter #1).
3. To date ({{DD/MM/YYYY}}), the company's reply has been {{WHAT THEY ACTUALLY DID — "nothing" / "an automated reply" / "a letter denying the refund"}}.

Everything I want you to review is in these three attachments. I want to keep this short and precise, so I'll stop describing and let the documents do the rest.

REMEDY REQUEST: {{ONE REMEDY AND NUMBER — "a full refund of ${{AMOUNT}}"}}.

Please reply by {{DATE +7 DAYS}}. If I don't hear from you by then, my next steps are (a) filing a formal complaint with {{NAMED REGULATOR/OMBUDSMAN}} and (b) assessing what remains available to me in small claims — in that order, and only because the earlier route ran out first.

Attachments:
1. Letter #1, dated {{DATE}}
2. Receipt / proof of the deal
3. Call log and reference #s

Contact: {{EMAIL-OR-PHONE}}

I appreciate that complaints come in on businesses every day and things slip. If you or your reviewer needs any further records from me, just write to me at the address above.

{{YOUR NAME}}
```
