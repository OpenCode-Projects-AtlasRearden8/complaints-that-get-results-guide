# Template — Insurer: Denial Appeal (First Appeal Letter)

> Fill every `{{slot}}`. Deadlines here are short — Chapter 4's calendar is
> mandatory for insurance disputes. Check your denial letter for the appeal
> window BEFORE you write.

```
Date:           {{DAY-MONTH-YEAR}}
To:             {{INSURER NAME}} — Appeals Department ({{ADDRESS-or-PORTAL}})
From:           {{YOUR NAME}}, policy {{POLICY-####}}, claim {{CLAIM-####}}
Subject:        Appeal of denial — claim {{CLAIM-####}} — reply requested by {{DATE +14 DAYS}}

Dear Appeals Department,

I'm appealing the denial of my claim {{CLAIM-####}} dated {{DENIAL DATE}}, and I'm asking that the claim be reviewed again in full.

Facts:
1. My policy with you covers {{WHAT WAS COVERED, IN YOUR OWN PHRASING FROM THE POLICY}}.
2. The denial letter states the reason as {{EXACT WORDING FROM THE LETTER}}. I'd like to address that directly.
3. Here is why I believe the claim fits the coverage: {{YOUR FACTS — the annual timeline, the professional's letter, the records — point by point, one line each.}}
4. Please send copies of the policy provisions relied upon in the denial, as well as the applicable internal guidelines, so I can be sure I'm responding to the right version.

Attached, in support:
1. {{EVIDENCE ITEM}}
2. {{EVIDENCE ITEM}}
3. {{EVIDENCE ITEM}}

REMEDY REQUEST: A full re-review of claim {{CLAIM-####}} under the coverage in my policy {{POLICY-####}}, and written confirmation of the decision either way.

Could you also confirm, in your reply, the external review options that apply to my claim? I'd like to move straight to the appropriate next step if the internal review doesn't resolve it.

Please reply by {{DATE +14 DAYS}}, and reference claim {{CLAIM-####}} in the subject line.

Thank you — I'd rather sort this out with you than have to take the external route, and I appreciate a careful second look.

{{YOUR NAME}}
```

(Did you catch the note at number 4? Read every line before sending — and keep dates exact: appeal windows run from the date printed on the denial letter, not the day you first heard about it.)
