> case numbers — Chapter 2, utilities ladder.

```
Date:           {{DAY-MONTH-YEAR}}

From:           {{YOUR NAME}}, account {{ACCT-####}}, {{SERVICE ADDRESS}}
Subject:        Billing dispute — account {{ACCT-####}} — bill {{INVOICE REF}}

Dear Billing Disputes Team,

I'm disputing the amount billed for my {{ELECTRIC / WATER / GAS / INTERNET}} service for the period {{BILLING PERIOD}}, and asking for a review.

Facts:
1. The bill dated {{BILL DATE}} charged ${{AMOUNT}}, roughly {{COMPARISON: "10× the amount of my previous bill / usage in June"}}.
2. The reading used was {{ESTIMATED OR ACTUAL, from the bill}}, and my usage history (attached) shows consumption of {{TYPICAL PREVIOUS FIGURE}} in comparable periods.
3. This billing is more than double anything this account has ever produced, and no one I've contacted has explained it.

REMEDY REQUEST: Please review the reading on bill {{INVOICE REF}} — including an updated meter read if needed — and issue a corrected invoice.

I've attached my usage history and the two bills. My payment history on this account is unchanged and up to date, and I'd like to get back to that.

Please reply by {{DATE +14 DAYS}}, ideally with the case/reference number for this dispute so I can follow up properly. If I don't hear from you, I'll take the next step and raise this with the {{LOCAL UTILITY REGULATOR, BY NAME IF YOU KNOW IT}}.

Contact: {{EMAIL-OR-PHONE}}

Thank you — I appreciate you looking into this.

{{YOUR NAME}}
```
